About Me

I am a 2024-entry undergraduate in Software Engineering at Chongqing University, where I am ranked first in my major. My work explores how testing, program analysis, and intelligent techniques can expose subtle defects in real software systems.

I am especially interested in testing static analyzers and vector database systems, where reliable behavior depends on reasoning beyond conventional test oracles. I enjoy turning observations from real engineering practice into reproducible research methods.
